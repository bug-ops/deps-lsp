packages.lock.json parser.

Every field except the package name is optional (S2): "type": "Project" and "type": "CentralTransitive" entries carry requested but no resolved at all — a required resolved field would abort deserialization of the entire file, which would only surface on multi-project solutions and not on the single-project fixture a test is most likely to use. Entries without resolved are simply skipped.

packages.<project_name>.lock.json cannot be expressed here — lockfile_filenames() is an exact-name list with no glob support (D3, follow-up).

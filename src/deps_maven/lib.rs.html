3// limit is occasionally insufficient to prove the resulting `Send` bound and downgrades a
4// previously-silent trait-solver retry into `recursion_depth_exceeding_limit`, which the fuzz

6// class of fix as deps-cargo (#745), deps-nuget (#696), deps-swift (#673), deps-composer.
7// deps-gradle delegates version lookups to this registry and shares the same exposure.
